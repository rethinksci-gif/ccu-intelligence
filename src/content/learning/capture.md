Capture separates CO₂ from a mixture. Absorption transfers CO₂ into a liquid; adsorption binds it on a solid; membranes exploit selective permeation; cryogenic approaches exploit phase behavior. Regeneration, purification and compression then determine whether the stream meets a downstream process specification. [DOE CCUS technology overview](https://www.energy.gov/fe/downloads/accelerating-breakthrough-innovation-carbon-capture-utilization-and-storage)

## Compare the full delivery boundary

A capture-only cost excludes some combination of conditioning, compression, transport and storage. Direct air capture starts from a much more dilute stream than many industrial sources. Feed composition, contaminants, required purity, heat quality and plant integration all affect the appropriate separation route. [IEA, *Direct Air Capture*](https://www.iea.org/reports/direct-air-capture-2022/executive-summary)

For each reported performance figure, record:

- Inlet composition and CO₂ concentration.
- Capture fraction and product purity as separate metrics.
- Heat and electricity use with units and boundary.
- Solvent or sorbent replacement and test duration.
- Delivered pressure, distance and available infrastructure.

**Analyst reading rule:** do not compare USD per tonne captured with USD per tonne delivered unless the missing steps are reconciled. A high capture fraction does not establish low cost or low lifecycle emissions.

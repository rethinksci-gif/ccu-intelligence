Lifecycle assessment compares environmental burdens for a stated function and boundary. For CCU, CO₂ sourcing, energy supply, coproduct allocation, displaced production and the product's eventual fate can materially change the answer. [AssessCCUS LCA process](https://assessccus.globalco2initiative.org/lca/detailed-process/)

## Make the comparison reproducible

Record the functional unit, geography, year, reference scenario, allocation method, system boundary and data confidence. Cradle-to-gate and cradle-to-grave results cannot be compared as if they covered the same stages. Report sensitivity to electricity sourcing and end-of-life assumptions.

Avoid counting the same captured tonne as both an avoided upstream emission and a removal credit without a consistent accounting framework. Atmospheric origin and durable retention are necessary considerations for removal, while fossil-source utilization may instead substitute fossil feedstock or defer release.

**Analyst reading rule:** ask for a carbon-flow diagram and the counterfactual. If the product releases CO₂ during use, that release cannot simply disappear beyond a conveniently chosen boundary. Net benefit remains unverified until the complete comparison supports it.
